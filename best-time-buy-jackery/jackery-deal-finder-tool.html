When to Expect the Best Jackery Deals

Jackery products, known for their reliability and performance, frequently go on sale during major retail events. Knowing when to look can save you a significant amount of money.

Black Friday & Cyber Monday (November)

Historically, these are the biggest sales events of the year. Expect substantial discounts on a wide range of Jackery models, often bundled with solar panels. Start looking from early November through the first week of December.

Amazon Prime Day (July)

Amazon's annual Prime Day event is another prime opportunity for deals. While not exclusive to Amazon, many retailers match or offer competing sales. Look for deals on popular models like the Jackery Explorer 500 or 1000.

Spring & Fall Sales (March-April, September-October)

Keep an eye out for seasonal sales, especially around outdoor activity peaks. Jackery often offers promotions to align with camping, RVing, and emergency preparedness needs.

Holiday Sales (Memorial Day, Labor Day, Christmas)

Smaller, but still significant, discounts can be found during other public holidays. These are good times to snag a deal if you missed the larger events.

Tips for Finding the Best Jackery Discounts

  • Official Jackery Website: Always check the official Jackery website for direct sales and refurbished units.
  • Sign Up for Newsletters: Subscribe to Jackery's email list and those of major retailers (Amazon, Best Buy, etc.) for early access to deals.
  • Follow Social Media: Jackery often announces flash sales or special promotions on their social media channels.
  • Price Tracking Tools: Use tools like Keepa or CamelCamelCamel to track historical prices on Amazon and set up price drop alerts.
  • Bundle Deals: Look for bundles that include a power station and solar panels, as these often offer greater overall savings.

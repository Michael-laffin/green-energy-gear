How it Works

This tool helps you estimate how long a Jackery portable power station can run your high-wattage appliances. The calculation is based on the Jackery's battery capacity (Wh) and the running wattage of your appliance. Keep in mind that actual runtime may vary due to factors like appliance efficiency, battery degradation, and environmental conditions.

For optimal performance and to ensure compatibility, always check your appliance's specific power requirements. If your appliance has a high peak (surge) wattage, ensure the Jackery model you choose can handle that initial power draw.
